Privacy Policy for BoardQues

Last updated: March 12, 2026

At BoardQues, accessible from boardques.vercel.app, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by BoardQues and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We value your privacy. We do not require you to provide personal information to browse our site. We may collect personal information such as your name or email address only if you contact us directly via email or through any provided contact forms.

2. Log Files

BoardQues follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting companies do this as part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, BoardQues uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

5. Advertising Partners

Some of the advertisers on our site may use cookies and web beacons. Our advertising partner is Google AdSense. Each of our advertising partners has their own Privacy Policy for their policies on user data.

6. Children's Information

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity.

7. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at jariftahmid10@gmail.com.
