Welcome to the Teacher's Corner

We believe that teachers are the backbone of the educational ecosystem. The Teacher's Corner is a dedicated space designed to support, empower, and collaborate with the educators who are shaping the future of Bangladesh. Here, you will find resources to supplement your classroom teaching, tools to engage your students, and opportunities to share your expertise with a wider audience. We recognize the immense effort you put into your work, and this space is our way of providing you with modern tools to make your job more effective and rewarding. Whether you are looking for new ways to explain a complex topic, seeking high-quality materials for a mock test, or wishing to contribute to a national educational repository, you are in the right place.

Empowering Educators: Resources for the Modern Classroom

BoardQues is more than a student-centric platform; it is a powerful ally for teachers. Our extensive database of board questions and curated academic content can be seamlessly integrated into your teaching methodology. Here are some ways you can leverage our platform to create a more dynamic and effective learning environment:

  • Create Custom Assignments and Mock Tests: Use our vast question bank to create tailored homework assignments, chapter-end quizzes, and full-fledged mock tests. You can filter questions by year, board, and subject to perfectly match your teaching plan and help students practice with real exam questions.
  • Implement the Flipped Classroom Model: Encourage your students to watch our video tutorials or read our in-depth articles on a topic before the class. This allows you to use precious classroom time for more interactive activities like problem-solving sessions, discussions, and collaborative projects, rather than just delivering lectures.
  • Enhance In-Class Engagement: Project a question from our platform and solve it together in class. Use our articles to spark a debate or discussion on a challenging concept. Our resources can serve as an excellent starting point for engaging activities that cater to different learning styles.
  • Remedial and Advanced Support: Easily direct your students to specific resources based on their needs. For students who are struggling with a concept, our simplified articles can provide the extra support they need. For advanced learners, you can assign challenging questions from different boards to keep them engaged and motivated.

Join Us: Content Contribution & Collaboration

Are you passionate about your subject? Do you have a knack for explaining difficult concepts in a simple and engaging way? If so, we invite you to become a part of our mission. We are building a community of educators who are committed to creating a high-quality, free, and accessible educational repository for all students in Bangladesh.

Why Collaborate with BoardQues?

Contributing to BoardQues is an opportunity to extend your impact far beyond the walls of your classroom. By sharing your knowledge and resources, you can help thousands of students across the country. It is also a chance for professional growth and recognition. Your contributions will be featured on our platform, acknowledging your expertise and dedication to education.

What We're Looking For:

We are always looking for talented educators to help us expand our content. We welcome contributions in various forms:

  • New and Unique Questions: Share your self-created questions that test conceptual understanding.
  • Detailed Solutions: Provide step-by-step solutions to existing board questions.
  • In-Depth Articles: Write articles on specific topics, exam strategies, or study techniques.
  • Video Tutorials: If you create educational videos, we would love to feature them.

How to Contribute:

If you are interested in contributing, please reach out to us through our Contact Us page with the subject line "Content Contribution". Tell us a bit about yourself and how you would like to collaborate. Our team will get in touch with you to discuss the next steps. All submitted content will be reviewed by our editorial team to ensure it meets our quality standards before being published.
