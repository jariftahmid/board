How to Make the Most of This Page

Our question bank is a powerful tool, and using it effectively can significantly boost your preparation. Here’s a step-by-step guide to leveraging this resource:

  • Step 1: Select Your Exam Type: Begin by choosing whether you are preparing for the SSC or HSC examination. This initial filter ensures that all subsequent options are relevant to your specific curriculum.
  • Step 2: Choose Your Subject: Next, select the subject you wish to practice. Whether it's Math, Physics, English, or Chemistry, choosing a subject will narrow down the available papers and help you focus on one area at a time.
  • Step 3: Filter by Education Board: Different education boards (e.g., Dhaka, Rajshahi, Chittagong) can have slight variations in their question patterns. Selecting your specific board allows you to practice with the exact type of papers you will face in your exams.
  • Step 4: Pick the Year: Finally, select the year of the question paper you want to view. We recommend starting with the most recent year and working your way backward to get a sense of the latest trends and patterns.

By using these filters, you can create a highly customized and targeted practice session. For instance, you can focus on all Physics questions from the Dhaka board for the last three years to identify recurring topics and concepts.

Subject-Specific Study Guidance

While practicing is crucial for all subjects, the approach can differ. Here are some tips for tackling different subjects using our question bank:

Physics & Higher Math

For subjects that are heavy on problem-solving, focus on the application of formulas and concepts. Don't just look at a question and think you know how to solve it; actually solve it from start to finish. Pay close attention to the mark distribution for different steps and practice drawing neat, labeled diagrams, as they often carry marks.

Chemistry

Chemistry requires a balance of memorization (for reactions and properties) and understanding (for mechanisms and principles). Use past papers to identify which reactions and definitions are most frequently asked. Practice writing out chemical equations and drawing structures repeatedly to build muscle memory.

English & Bangla

For language-based subjects, focus on the grammar and writing sections. Go through past papers to compile a list of common grammar rules that are tested. For the writing parts (essays, paragraphs, letters), analyze the prompts and practice brainstorming and outlining your responses within a time limit. This will improve both your writing speed and quality.
